My project is an HR Management System developed using Node.js, Express.js, MySQL, HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

The main objective of the project is to automate HR-related activities such as employee management, attendance tracking, leave management, holiday management and notifications.

The system supports two roles: Admin and Employee. First, users log in using their Employee ID and password. Based on their role, they are redirected to their respective dashboard.

In the Employee Management module, admins can add, view and search employee details such as department, designation, contact information and employment details.

In the Leave Management module, employees can apply for leave and admins can approve or reject leave requests. I have also implemented validation to prevent duplicate or overlapping leave applications.

The Holiday Management module allows administrators to maintain the organization's holiday calendar.

Additionally, I implemented a Notification System that automatically sends notifications when leave requests are submitted, approved or rejected.

The backend is built using Express.js REST APIs and MySQL is used as the database for storing employee, attendance, leave and holiday records.

My project is Combat Calc, a smart boxing headgear designed to improve fairness and safety in combat sports.

The main problem we identified was that boxing matches often depend on human judgment, which can lead to biased scoring and missed injury detection. To solve this, we developed a wearable system that can automatically detect punch force, punch direction, and head impacts in real time.

The hardware consists of an Arduino Uno, MPU6050 accelerometer and gyroscope, FSR sensors, Bluetooth module, buzzer, and LED indicators. The FSR sensors detect punch force from different directions, while the MPU6050 tracks head movement and possible knockback events.

When a punch is detected, the sensor data is processed by the microcontroller and transmitted wirelessly through Bluetooth. High-impact events trigger alerts and can be used for objective scoring and athlete safety monitoring.

Through this project, I gained experience in embedded systems, sensor integration, hardware debugging, wireless communication, and real-time data processing. The future goal is to integrate AI analytics and advanced injury prediction features for professional sports applications.

Interviewer Follow-up Questions

Q: Why did you choose this project?

Boxing scoring is often subjective. We wanted to create a low-cost wearable solution that provides objective and data-driven scoring while improving athlete safety.

Q: What is your contribution?

I worked on sensor integration, hardware setup, testing, data collection, debugging, and validating the punch detection workflow.

Q: Why MPU6050?

MPU6050 contains both an accelerometer and gyroscope, allowing us to measure movement, orientation, and head impacts accurately.

Q: Why FSR sensors?

FSR (Force Sensitive Resistor) sensors can detect pressure and impact force, making them suitable for punch detection.

Q: Challenges faced?

Sensor calibration, reducing false detections, maintaining comfort inside the headgear, and ensuring durability during repeated impacts.

Q: Future Enhancement?

AI-based punch classification, injury prediction, cloud analytics, mobile app integration, and automatic match scoring.
